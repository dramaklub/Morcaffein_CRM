====================================================================
SCOPE NOTES
====================================================================
- No inventory tracking. This system is POS-only — it records sales
  and awards loyalty points; it does not decrement stock, warn about
  low inventory, or track ingredient quantities. "In stock / out of
  stock" states do not exist as a concept.
- No coffee drinks were in the original seed data; this spec adds
  them. The shop's identity is matcha-forward, but the menu
  includes a full coffee and iced-coffee lineup for breadth.
- Hot/Iced is expressed as SEPARATE PRODUCTS, not as a modifier.
  Each temp-optional drink exists as two product rows labelled with
  a (Hot) or (Cold) suffix. This is a deliberate choice to keep the
  POS fast — tapping a drink card then a size adds directly to the
  cart without any modal, and no Hot/Iced modifier has to be picked
  mid-sale.
- Size pricing is UNIFORM across the entire menu: Regular ₱39,
  Large ₱49, XL ₱59. Every drink has all three sizes, matcha
  included. There is no per-category or per-product size exception.
- Add-ons are UNIVERSAL: every modifier is available on every
  drink. Nothing is restricted per-product or per-category at the
  data level. The UI groups modifiers by category for scannability
  (Milk Swaps / Extra Shots / Syrups / Toppings / Prep) but this
  grouping is DISPLAY-ONLY — it gates nothing.
- Add-on pricing is FLAT per add-on. No size-tiered pricing. ₱0 is
  a valid price for prep modifiers.
- There are NO default modifiers. Every add-on is opt-in; the order
  line only records what the staff explicitly selects.

====================================================================
DELIBERATE DECISIONS — WHY
====================================================================
Captured so future readers understand these aren't defaults that
just happened; they're choices.

1. Hot/Iced as products, not a modifier.
   Rationale: POS speed. A "Hot/Iced" modifier would force every
   temp-optional drink through the item-customizer modal (because
   the quick-add size buttons on the drink card can't express a
   modifier choice), adding a step to the most common sale. Two
   products per temp keeps the quick-add flow intact: tap card, tap
   size, done.

2. Uniform size pricing.
   Rationale: simplicity. Per-category or per-product pricing
   schemes add branching to the UI (the drink card has to know
   whether to show 2 or 3 size buttons) and to the seed data. A
   single 3-tier price applied uniformly removes both branches.
   Business accepts that matcha costs more to produce than it's
   priced at, as a menu-simplification trade.

3. Universal modifiers.
   Rationale: staff discretion over system enforcement. A matcha
   drink with an "Extra Espresso Shot" is unusual but not illegal —
   the shop would rather let staff make that call than build and
   maintain a product→modifier mapping. The category grouping in
   the UI makes the relevant modifiers easy to find, so nonsensical
   combinations are unlikely to be picked by mistake.

4. Flat modifier pricing.
   Rationale: menu legibility. Size-tiered modifier pricing (Oat
   Milk +₱20 on Regular, +₱30 on Large) multiplies the modifier
   table's size and adds cognitive load at the register. Flat
   pricing means "Oat Milk is +₱20" is true for every drink, every
   size.

5. ₱0 prep modifiers.
   Rationale: the POS should record how a drink was made, not just
   what it cost. "Less Ice" and "Half Sugar" being ₱0 doesn't make
   them worthless data — they're part of the order record and
   matter if a customer disputes what they received.

6. No inventory tracking.
   Rationale: this is a POS + loyalty system, not a stock system.
   Ingredient consumption, low-stock warnings, and reorder points
   are out of scope. Products are either on the menu or they aren't;
   "is this drink currently available?" is not modeled.

WHAT'S STILL OPEN (not decided here)

These are known but deliberately left for later:

- Whether "Iced Matcha" appears as a distinct cold non-coffee
  listing or is only reachable via the matcha series. Resolution:

  non-coffee entry duplicates it.
